How it works

Three stages. The first two are fully local — only clean, extracted tasks ever leave your machine.

1Local

🎧 Whisper

Your speech becomes text on-device. Nothing is uploaded, ever.

→
2Local

🧠 Local model

Diarizes who said what (voice + male/female timbre), tags importance, cuts the stream into tasks, enriches a vector DB, and builds your board.

→
3Gonka

⚡ Kimi-K2.6

Decentralized open-weight AI executes the tasks. Paid in GNK. The only stage that touches the network — and only with clean, redacted tasks.

What makes it different

A second brain that hears context, extracts work, and gets it done — without handing your life to a cloud provider.

🔒

Secrets never leave

A double-redaction barrier blocks seed phrases, private keys, cards, and passwords before anything is sent out. Critical secrets hard-stop locally.

👥

Knows who's talking

Local voiceprint (SpeechBrain ECAPA) recognizes owner vs. others, plus male/female timbre — tasks are attributed to the right speaker.

🎙️

Always-on, legal

Native iOS app captures in the background over an encrypted Tailscale tunnel to your own Mac. The orange mic indicator stays on — you're always in control.

⚡

Cheap execution

Tasks run on Gonka's open-weight Kimi-K2.6, billed in GNK at network cost — a fraction of centralized API prices.

🧩

OpenClaw plugins

Ships an OpenClaw plugin exposing ingest / memory-search / task-board / orchestrator tools, so your agents plug straight in.

🛠️

Run from scratch

Fully open source. One-command install on a clean Mac mini. Your data, your machine, your keys.

Private by architecture, not by promise

Raw audio, transcripts, memory, vectors, and audit logs stay on your computer. The local model never executes tasks — it only organizes text. Gonka is the only execution backend, and it only ever sees clean, redacted tasks.
